# Sample Submission Guideline for Mass Spectrometry (Novogene AMEA)

Source document: `submission_LCMS.docx`  
Last updated: March 2025  
Scope: AMEA (excluding mainland China)

## 1) Sample requirement for metabolomics

### 1.1 Lab acceptance scope
- Untargeted metabolomics: Singapore lab starts accepting listed sample types from 2025-04-01.
- Lipidomics: document states China lab acceptance.

### 1.2 Required amount/volume (core list)
| Sample type | Requirement |
|---|---:|
| Human & animal tissue | 200 mg |
| Plant tissue | 500 mg |
| Plasma / Serum | 200 μL |
| Feces / Intestinal contents | 200 mg |
| Saliva | 200 μL |
| Urine | 200 μL |
| Cell | ≥10^7 |

### 1.3 China-lab-only examples in document
| Sample type | Requirement |
|---|---:|
| Amniotic fluid / Bile / Tear etc. | 200 μL |
| Supernatant / Fermentation broth | 2 mL |
| Microbial thallus | 100 μL |
| Microbial solution | 2 mL |
| Culture medium | 200 mg |

### 1.4 General packaging note
- Use 1.5 mL centrifuge tubes for sample collection where applicable.
- Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
- Freeze in liquid nitrogen (typically 15 min), store at -80°C, and ship with sufficient dry ice.

## 2) Sample preparation for metabolomics (by sample type)

### 2.1 Human & animal tissue
- Remove blood contamination as much as possible.
- Aliquot to ~200 mg/sample, label clearly.
- Snap-freeze in liquid nitrogen (≥15 min), store at -80°C, ship on dry ice.
- Keep sampling location consistent across biological replicates.

### 2.2 Plant samples
#### Leaves / stems / flowers
- Wrap immediately, label clearly, snap-freeze, then -80°C storage.
- Keep sampling conditions consistent within same group.

#### Roots
- Rinse soil quickly with PBS.
- Collect target region, ~500 mg/sample, snap-freeze, then -80°C.

#### Fruits / seeds
- Large juicy fruit: cut to uniform pieces (~500 mg/sample), aliquot, snap-freeze.
- Small seeds: mix within same group before aliquoting (~500 mg/sample).
- Whole fruit option: pack in 50 mL tube or sealed bag, then snap-freeze.

### 2.3 Plasma / serum
#### Plasma
- Recommended: heparin sodium anticoagulant tube.
- Centrifuge at 3000 rpm, 4°C, 10 min.
- Aliquot upper layer to 0.2 mL/tube.

#### Serum
- Use non-anticoagulant vacuum tube.
- Coagulate/stratify (e.g., ~1 hour at room temperature or 37°C), then centrifuge.
- Aliquot 0.2 mL/tube.

#### Notes
- Keep pre-analytical handling consistent (timing, centrifugation conditions).
- Severe hemolysis may make samples unsuitable.

### 2.4 Feces / intestinal contents
- Aliquot ~200 mg/tube immediately after collection.
- Freeze immediately in liquid nitrogen, store -80°C.
- Fast microbial metabolism requires rapid handling.

### 2.5 Saliva
- Suggested collection window in document: morning (e.g., 9:30–11:30).
- Rinse mouth, collect saliva on ice, avoid sputum contamination.
- Centrifuge and aliquot supernatant; optional filtration (0.22 μm) is noted.
- Pre-collection restrictions in document include no smoking/drinking/eating shortly before sampling.

### 2.6 Urine
- Midstream urine (clinical) or 1-hour morning urine (animal) is noted.
- Example condition: centrifuge at 3000 rpm, 4°C, 10 min, then aliquot (e.g., 500 μL/tube).
- For 24-hour urine, document notes low-temperature collection setup and sodium azide precautions.

### 2.7 Tears
- Document describes Schirmer strip-based collection workflow.
- Suggested morning collection and same-day per-eye frequency limit are noted.

### 2.8 Microbe samples
- Includes thallus, liquid culture medium, and solid culture medium workflows.
- Wash with pre-chilled PBS where applicable.
- Keep bacterial amount and handling timing consistent.
- OD600 0.6–0.8 is mentioned as a common log-phase reference.

### 2.9 Cell samples
- Separate workflows for suspension cells, adherent cells, and culture medium.
- Keep cell count consistent across samples.
- For extracellular metabolism studies, document notes serum-free medium considerations.

## 3) Untargeted metabolomics extraction method (document section summary)

### 3.1 Tissue sample
- Example in document: 100 mg tissue, pre-chilled 80% methanol extraction, cold centrifugation, supernatant cleanup, LC-MS/MS injection.

### 3.2 Liquid sample
- Example in document: 100 μL sample + pre-chilled methanol extraction, cold centrifugation and cleanup, LC-MS/MS injection.

### 3.3 Cell or bacteria sample
- Methanol-based extraction, vortex/sonication/centrifugation steps, reconstitution before LC-MS/MS.
