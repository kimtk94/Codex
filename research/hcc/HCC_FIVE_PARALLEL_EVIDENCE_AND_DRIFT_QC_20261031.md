## Published radiologist/source study evidence — important scientific distinction

- Gross et al., *Data in Brief* **51:109662** (2023), DOI [10.1016/j.dib.2023.109662](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109662). Source dataset: [LiverHccSeg v1.1](https://zenodo.org/records/8179129).
- The study's **two board-certified abdominal radiologists** produced original lesion masks and provided HCC segmentation agreement metrics. Scans were curated so that **when multiple studies were available, pretreatment studies were prioritized** and image quality was considered. Thus source mask annotation is radiologist-origin, not an unreviewed arbitrary autogenerated lesion mask.
- What this does **not** resolve: a signed independent re-reading of the *selected index lesion* used in our project, identification that this radiological lesion corresponds to the resected tissue aliquot, or proof that MRI was acquired before **every** treatment mentioned in GDC. Reconciliation of GDC-relative and TCIA shifted dates remains prohibited without a justified source-level mapping.
- Therefore HCC-12 and HCC-14 retain **PARTIAL** status: published expert annotation is technical evidence, not clinical paired-patient inclusion signoff.

## Immutable technical source drift anchor

- [Baseline JSON](HCC_TECHNICAL_SNAPSHOT_BASELINE_20261031.json) is a frozen **14-patient risk-label snapshot** from original verified run #154 (not clinician approved, not registered clinical protocol).
- [Drift and unauthorized promotion validator](ci/mri_parallel_snapshot_diff.py) checks the actual 14-patient joint CSV, public source metrics, and GO/HOLD/STOP summary against the frozen snapshot. It verifies all subjects and correctly preserves A5W4's other-organ surgery instead of silently upgrading it to HCC-targeting therapy.
- A legitimate new source correction should first produce a **review-required failure**, with side-by-side changed patient fields in `hcc_batch_patient_source_drift_report.json`, then be human-reviewed and committed with an updated source/evidence basis. Do not have CI auto-approve the new clinical status, silently edit the baseline or regenerate a biological result.
- This is a **fixed-source consistency/drift detector**, not continuous remote surveillance for new diagnoses, TSS information or clinical study annotations. Remote official XLSX is rechecked per PR; official GDC source checks are preserved from evidence runs and their source hashes.
